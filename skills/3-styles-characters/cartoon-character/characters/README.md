# Sample characters

Two ready rigs ship so you can try the skills before making your own.

- **`ali/`**: a cartoon of Ali, who made this pack. He made it with an image
  model from his own photos and shares it for trying these skills.
- **`stashy/`**: Stashy, the [FavStash](https://www.favstash.app) mascot.

**Terms.** These two characters are not covered by the repository's Apache-2.0
licence. You may use them to try, learn and demo these skills, including in videos
you post about doing so. Don't use them as your own brand's character, to speak for
or impersonate Ali or FavStash, or to imply that either endorses you or your
product. For your channel, make your own character with
[make a character](../references/make-a-character.md).
