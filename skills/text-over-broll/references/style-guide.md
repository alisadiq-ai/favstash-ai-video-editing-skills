# Text-over-B-roll style guide

## Strong source material

This format works when the text can be understood silently, the idea has an emotional or practical turn, and the available visuals can do more than decorate it. It is weak for dense tutorials, nuanced disclaimers, and ideas that depend on vocal charisma.

## Useful structures

- **Recognition → reframe:** name a familiar feeling, then give it a sharper interpretation.
- **Mistake → consequence → replacement:** useful for educational or founder content.
- **Specific moment → universal point:** let one concrete detail earn the larger statement.
- **Three-beat list:** each item must advance, contrast, or escalate; avoid arbitrary list length.

Write for the eye. A beat often holds 3–12 words, but syntax and reading difficulty matter more than a fixed count. Give a dense or surprising line longer than a familiar phrase.

## Match footage by role

- `establish`: where and when;
- `action`: visible progress or struggle;
- `detail`: texture and intimacy;
- `proof`: the product/result/behavior being claimed;
- `contrast`: before/after or expectation/reality;
- `breath`: a quieter shot before the payoff;
- `closure`: visual completion or open-ended reflection.

Avoid matching every noun literally. A metaphorical visual can be stronger when it remains understandable. Prefer continuity in color, direction, lighting, or movement across adjacent shots.

## Text treatment

Use one core alignment and a small set of emphasis devices: weight, accent color, a word reveal, or a position shift. Highlight the semantic pivot, not random keywords. Keep the background readable with crop choice first, then a gradient/panel/shadow if needed.

For calm work, let 2–5 shots carry a 10–20 second piece. For energetic work, use more changes only when the visuals remain interpretable. A hold can be a deliberate beat.

## Common failures

- inspirational copy so broad that it could sit over any video;
- beautiful footage that contradicts the text's emotion;
- too many fonts, colors, or entrance effects;
- text changing before an average viewer can read it;
- placing the payoff under platform controls;
- cloning a saved reel's exact copy, shot order, or music.
