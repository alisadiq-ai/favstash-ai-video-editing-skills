# Screen-demo guide

## Demonstration spine

Open with the outcome, pain, or visible payoff. Show only the steps required to believe the result. A useful sequence is `problem state → decisive action(s) → observable result → optional next step`.

## Desktop-to-vertical treatment

- Do not letterbox an unreadable full desktop unless it is a momentary establishing view.
- Reframe around the active region, or place the screen in a designed panel and cut to magnified details.
- Preserve orientation: make crop moves traceable and avoid teleporting between unrelated UI regions.
- Hold result states long enough to inspect.

## Cursor and touch

Keep the pointer visible but not oversized unless the brand/style calls for it. Ease toward targets, land precisely, show a restrained click/tap response, and move only when the next action begins. On mobile captures, use a tap ring or brief highlight rather than inventing a desktop cursor.

## Privacy and truth

Blur or replace email addresses, names, tokens, private URLs, notifications, and customer information. Verify redaction across every moving frame. Do not animate a fake success state while presenting it as real captured behavior; label mockups or conceptual sequences when needed.

## Audio and copy

Explain intent and consequence, not every mechanical click. Let UI feedback be audible only when useful and licensed. Use the clean-click SFX as a subtle augmentation rather than stacking it over loud native sounds.
