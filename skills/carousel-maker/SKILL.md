# Carousel Maker

Make every slide earn the swipe. A carousel is a paced narrative, not a long article chopped into rectangles.

## Workflow

1. Read [`references/carousel-guide.md`](references/carousel-guide.md) and the creator's `.favstash-studio/preferences.md`.
2. Create a dated run with style `carousel-maker`; use a 1080×1350 canvas by default and adjust when the destination needs another ratio.
3. Define the reader promise and the final payoff/action. Outline the sequence before styling.
4. Choose a restrained layout family from preferences or adapt [`assets/carousel-starter.html`](assets/carousel-starter.html). Do not imitate another creator's distinctive design exactly.
5. Render numbered PNGs such as `01-cover.png` through `08-close.png` into `exports/`. Keep editable HTML/CSS/data in `work/`.
6. Inspect every slide at phone size and as a contact sheet. Check hierarchy, reading load, contrast, alignment, image rights, and continuity.
7. Write per-slide alt text plus a caption draft in `reports/delivery.md`.
8. If FavStash is connected and publishing/planning is requested, prepare the ordered media and draft. Obtain exact confirmation before scheduling or publishing.

Use the platform's current allowed image count rather than assuming one permanent maximum. If the story needs too many slides, split it or tighten it.
