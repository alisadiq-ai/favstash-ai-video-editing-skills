# Short-Form Sound Design

Build attention through contrast and clarity. More effects do not automatically create more energy.

## Workflow

1. Read [`references/mixing-guide.md`](references/mixing-guide.md) and the shared [rights guide](../favstash-shortform/references/rights-and-reference-media.md).
2. Work inside the active edit run. Ledger voice, music, native sound, room tone, and every external SFX with source and rights.
3. Establish intelligible voice and clean edits first. Then place music for emotional shape; automate/duck it around speech and important quiet moments.
4. Choose effects from [`assets/sfx/manifest.json`](assets/sfx/manifest.json) by narrative role. Audition in context and vary or omit repeated cues.
5. Use native UI/foley only when it is owned/approved and helps credibility. Do not layer a generated click on top of an already clear native click without purpose.
6. Apply short fades at edits, remove accidental clipping, and preserve deliberate dynamics.
7. Review on phone speakers and headphones at a normal listening level. Watch the full ending for abrupt cuts or dangling reverb.

Extracted music/audio from a reference remains analysis-only unless the user explicitly confirms reusable rights through the repository script. Platform availability or yt-dlp downloadability is not a license.
